Browsers cannot list nearby Wi-Fi networks, their names or their signal strength; that needs a native app. This radar plots your real connection: each point is a ping to the server hosting this page (distance from the centre = latency, red marks = lost packets).

The phone magnetometer (Chrome on Android) measures the magnetic field in microtesla; motors, transformers, magnets and mains cables raise it. The hum detector listens through the microphone for 50/60 Hz mains buzz. Both are indicators only: they cannot tell which appliance it is.
